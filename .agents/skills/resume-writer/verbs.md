# Approved Resume Action Verbs

Use this list as a suggestion bank for precise past-tense bullet openings. Prefer the candidate's accurate verb over a forced synonym, even when that verb is not listed. Before finalizing, scan adjacent bullets for distracting repetition and vary an opener only when a natural, equally accurate alternative exists.

## Choose Verbs By Skill Category

When useful, choose the bullet-leading verb by the strongest skill signal the bullet proves:

- **Technical** (`developed`, `engineered`, `implemented`, `maintained`, `operated`, `designed`) — systems, platforms, pipelines, APIs, containers, architecture.
- **Problem solving / research** (`resolved`, `diagnosed`, `identified`, `investigated`, `analyzed`) — incidents, debugging, root cause, observability, bottlenecks.
- **Management / organizational** (`led`, `coordinated`, `documented`, `systematized`, `planned`, `organized`) — leadership, delivery coordination, handoff, governance, repeatable operations.
- **Communication** (`communicated`, `interpreted`, `authored`, `wrote`) — stakeholder requirements, business-logic definition, documentation.
- **Result** (`improved`, `increased`, `reduced`, `economized`) — only when the headline is cost, time, speed, throughput, or efficiency.

Vary opening verbs across adjacent bullets; if several bullets have reduction outcomes, vary the verb by the skill demonstrated and keep the reduction as the outcome. A second verb inside a bullet (`Designed and delivered`, `Developed and automated`) is fine sparingly when both actions are distinct and supported — never to sound bigger.
